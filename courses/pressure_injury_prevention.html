Pressure Injury Prevention, Management & Staging in Aged & Community Care

An allied health module on why and how pressure injuries develop, recognising and staging them, the equipment used to manage risk, the effect of incontinence on skin, and when and how to escalate. Wound dressing and wound bed management are outside allied health scope and are not covered here.

Aged Care & Community Practice
Estimated time: 25–30 min
Includes competency check
Aged Care Physiotherapy Pty Ltd — Internal clinical education resource for allied health staff. This module covers prevention, recognition, staging, equipment and escalation; wound bed management and dressing selection remain the responsibility of the nursing/wound care team.  |  Review as part of ACP clinical governance.
